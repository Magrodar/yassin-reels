# (F) Brief for the Speech-Language Pathologist (SLP) Advisor

Hand this document, together with docs A, B and E, to the SLP at the first meeting.

---

## 1. Who we are looking for

- A licensed Egyptian SLP (أخصائي تخاطب) with **regular caseload experience with 2–4-year-old late talkers**. Adult or school-age experience alone is not enough.
- Experience coaching parents, or with parent-implemented intervention approaches, is a strong plus.
- A native speaker of Cairene Egyptian Arabic.

## 2. What the product is (and is not)

- A short-video tool that a parent uses **together with** the child. It shows real footage and plays a real Egyptian voice (or the parent's own voice). It gives the child silence to respond, and it grows sentences one step at a time (word → 2 → 3 → 4 words).
- It is **educational**. It does not diagnose, screen or treat. The app's copy recommends a professional assessment for any child who seems behind.

## 3. The role

| | |
|---|---|
| Type | Paid advisor, fixed number of review sessions (not an employee, not on-call) |
| Estimated time | 3 review sessions of 2–3 hours + one optional half day on the pilot shoot + field-test observation (2–3 hours) |
| Budget | Doc D §6: 6,000–12,000 EGP for the pilot phase |
| Credit | Named in the app as content advisor, only if they agree after seeing the final pilot |
| Confidentiality | A short NDA covering the scripts and the plan |

> **Keep roles separate.** If the same SLP also assesses or treats a child in the founders' family, keep that clinical relationship in a **separate agreement**, with separate fees and separate sessions. The child's therapy decisions must never be shaped by the product's needs, and the product's decisions must not rest on one child's case.

## 4. What we ask them to review — in order, with gates

### Gate 1 — the 10 pilot ladders (blocks voice recording and the shoot)
Ladders R01, R02, R03, R06, R07, E01, A01, A02, E02, Q02 in doc A, and their clips in doc B.

For each ladder, please answer:
1. **Dialect:** does every line sound like a real Cairo home? Flag anything textbook-like (فصحى), too theatrical, or baby talk.
2. **Level steps:** is each level really *one* step above the previous one for a late talker? Is L4 too long?
3. **Word choice:** is the target word among the useful early words for this age? Is there a better word for the same function?
4. **Gender forms:** are the feminine forms right and complete?
5. **Prompts:** are the questions and cloze stems natural and answerable at each level (e.g. "الولد بيعمل إيه؟" for an L1 child)?
6. **Pause:** are 4s (COMPLETE), 5s (IMITATE) and 6s (ANSWER, TEMPTATION) right, or should the defaults differ?

### Gate 2 — the design principles (doc E)
- Is the IMITATE share (≈ 16% of clips) reasonable, or should direct "قول…" prompts be lower or higher?
- Is the TEMPTATION clip type used correctly?
- Is the rule "the app always re-models and never judges" right?
- Are the parent coaching tips (brief §8) accurate and phrased well for Egyptian parents?

### Gate 3 — the remaining 56 ladders and the field-test protocol (before the v1 shoot)
- Full review of ladders 21–66, as in gate 1.
- Help design the field test (3–5 children aged 2–3.5):
  - inclusion criteria;
  - how to observe brief §11 questions 1–3 (watching to the end, imitation attempts, answers after 2–3 exposures);
  - a baseline and week-8 **10-minute play language sample** method for MLU;
  - whether a validated Egyptian-Arabic parent vocabulary checklist is available and usable;
  - consent and a referral path for any child whose parents may need a full assessment.

## 5. What we need back from them

- Written comments per ladder: **approve / change (with the new line) / drop**.
- One signed line per gate: "Reviewed and approved for filming/recording" (or not, with reasons).
- The changes are applied to `data/ladders.csv` and doc B, and each gate is recorded in the README decision log with the date.

## 6. Questions to ask in the first call (screening)

1. How many 2–4-year-old late talkers do you see in a typical month?
2. Which approach do you use to coach parents, and why?
3. What is your view on screen use at this age? (We are looking for a balanced, evidence-based answer, not a yes-man.)
4. Would you be comfortable being named as an advisor on an app? What would make you *not* comfortable?
5. Availability: can you complete gate 1 within 7–10 days?
