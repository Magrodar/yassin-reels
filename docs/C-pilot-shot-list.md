## 1. Technical and filming rules (apply to every shot)

**Camera**
- Shoot **vertical, native 9:16, 4K (2160×3840)**. Deliver 1080×1920. Do not shoot horizontal and crop, because that loses the close framing.
- **25 fps, shutter 1/50.** Egypt's mains are 50 Hz; 30 fps under home LEDs or fluorescent tubes gives flicker. Use 50 fps only for the E02 slow-motion shot.
- Lock exposure, focus and white balance per setup. No auto-exposure pumping.
- **POV height:** lens at 70–80 cm (a seated 3-year-old's eye line). The hand double's hands enter from the bottom of the frame.

**Coverage**
- Each action is shot twice: **A = wide** (the whole action and its context), **B = close** (the object fills 50–70% of the frame).
- The action must **finish inside the take**: the ball stops rolling, the glass is really empty.
- **Hold rule:** after every prompt beat, hold the frame **8 seconds, still**. This is the "your turn" footage the player stretches from 4 to 6 seconds. In IMITATE, the adult looks into the lens, smiles, raises the eyebrows slightly and **does not speak and does not nod** until the end of the hold.
- Two takes minimum of every A and B. With children, shoot until two usable takes exist, then move on.

**Speaking faces**
- A speaking mouth appears **only** in IMITATE clips (4 in the pilot). Everywhere else, adults are hands and torso; the face is cropped at the chin or out of frame. This keeps the audio replaceable by the parent's voice (doc E, change #2).
- IMITATE framing: head and shoulders, the mouth in the upper-middle third, the prop next to the face. Front light on the mouth; no shadow from a nose or hand.

**Sound**
- IMITATE: lavalier on the speaker, room tone recorded 30 s per setup.
- Everything else: record **clean natural sound** (pouring, glugging, slurping, the ball bouncing, the box rattling) with a second mic close to the action. These sounds carry meaning; no music is ever laid under them.
- Voice-over is **not** recorded on set. It is recorded separately as segments (see doc D).
- Say the clip ID aloud and clap at the start of each take.

**Set and wardrobe**
- Real apartment, decluttered: nothing on the counters behind the action, no TV on, no text or logos in frame.
- Wardrobe: plain, solid, muted colours (beige, grey, navy, olive). **No red** on anyone (the red ball must pop). No cartoon prints, no brand logos, no text on clothes.
- Safety: no glass near children (use clear hard plastic that looks like glass), no hot drinks, sharp edges padded, a stable table.
